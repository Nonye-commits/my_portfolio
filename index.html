About me

Product thinking rooted in human understanding

I started my career in education — a field that demands radical empathy, clear communication, and the patience to break down complexity until it clicks. Those instincts never left me. They became the foundation of how I think about product.

Today, I bring that same disposition to building digital products: I start with people, not features. I ask why before I ask what. And I know that the best solutions aren't always the most technically complex ones — they're the ones users actually trust and return to.

I've led product across EdTech, HealthTech, and community-driven platforms — collaborating with cross-functional teams, aligning stakeholders, and moving products from concept to launch with measurable impact.

My philosophy

Empathy first. Evidence always. Iteration forever.

Good product management starts with genuine curiosity about people — their needs, frustrations, and the workarounds they've quietly built because no one solved their problem properly. I lead with listening.

From there, I rely on data to pressure-test assumptions and structure thinking. I believe in shipping things that are incomplete enough to learn from, and iterating toward something that's excellent — not waiting for perfect before acting.

Ultimately, I measure success not by what shipped, but by what changed for the user. That's what drives me.
